## Production shortfalls

**Merged the shortfall session's fixes** (`claude/brave-yonath-05f458`): the validator models split producers (a
machine putting one item on two belt chains divides its output by inserter speed, not as planned) and the router
avoids them; crackers obey their circuit condition in game (`circuit_enabled`, read through a wired pipe). The
validator's lane-true producer split (newer, on this branch) and the split-producer shares now run in one max flow.

**The in-game verifier takes outputs at the planned rate by default** (`--uncapped` for the old behaviour), so a
factory cannot look good by over-making one pack while starving another. `--dump` adds every belt's lanes, inserter
and pipe to the report, and `tools/factorio-verify/starve.py <out>` traces each starving machine: the belts that
bring the missing item, upstream to who makes it, who else takes it on the way, and whether a producer standing
with full output can reach it at all.

**Why production science stayed at 1.81/s** in the re-solved 2/s factory (traced with starve.py on the shortfall
session's last in-game run): the two production-science machines at the end of the rail belt starve for rails,
while none of the four rail assemblers is blocked. Rails are short because the plan runs the rail and iron-stick
machines at exactly 100% of their speed (4 rail assemblers for exactly 10 crafts/s, 2 stick assemblers for exactly
5): any lost inserter swing upstream is lost for good. Block rebalancing also pushed machines to 100% (the
electric-furnace assemblers at 0.25/s of 0.25).

- New `planner.max_utilization`: machine counts round up past it and block rebalancing stops there. Default 0.97
  (see the community jobs below). The 2/s factory was re-solved with 0.97 overnight (`solves/two_sps_headroom`,
  1,261 machines against 1,220).

The first three were the split producers (fixed by the merge). Green 1/s is the 100% case; **max_utilization 0.97 is
now the default**. Tests that check hand-worked machine counts (and fixtures placed for them) use `handTuning`
(max_utilization 1). Green 22.5/s with beacons is a different fault: every machine is 20–30% short of ingredients
while the input belts are offered in full. The beaconed assemblers (16 crafts/s) are fed by one bulk inserter per
ingredient from belts that are not backed up; the inserter model (2.31 swings/s x hand 12 x belt derate 0.7 =
19.4/s) looks optimistic there.

**Bulk inserters from belts, measured.** One beaconed gear assembler fed by one bulk inserter (hand 12) from a full
express belt, outputs taken without limit: the inserter delivers 13.5 iron/s whatever the machine wants (19.4 in the
model). A bulk inserter waits for its hand to fill from the passing items: one swing plus 12 items at 0.58 of the
belt's throughput fits. With that in the inserter model the same job gets two inserters and makes its plan, and
green 22.5/s holds 22.5/s. Plain fast inserters are the other way round: one at hand 1 took 2.73/s from a belt (1.62
in the model), so the 0.7 belt derate is conservative for them (left as it is).

**Split producers at full speed.** The validator held a split producer at its planned rate and divided that by
inserter speed. In the game a machine whose outputs are all taken runs faster, so the division only bites at full
speed. With headroom every machine has spare speed, and the old model rejected sound factories (a gear assembler
feeding its own block and another one). The split now uses full speed as the machine's ceiling.

**Ring overlaps in factories.** Compaction could squeeze a block side to no padding while the block had heat pipes
on its ring, so two blocks' ring pipes landed on one tile (HeatTest failed now and then, OVERLAP or COLD). Sides
with anything on the ring keep a tile.

**Lane overflow in six-science factories** (the error the 2sc_job_2 resume died on, and the 2/s preview stitches
tonight): the validator's own fallback. When the lane-true producer split could not meet every draw of any one
item with producers at their planned rates, every item fell back to an arbitrary split, which piled one lane full
(38/s on a 30/s lane). Producers may now run up to full speed there, and only items without a lane-true split fall
back. The 2/s preview that failed with 12 overflow errors validates.

## Block ports on the wrong edge

Found in 9 of 10 factory runs in `solves/` (e.g. iron ore on the north edge of a job that says west). Cause: the
floorplan may turn a block, and a port that leaves the factory straight out of the block turns with it. A block
with such ports now only takes turns that keep each of them on an edge the job allows.

## Cells (clusters of clusters)

The four science machines inside the chemical-science cells starved in game (0.367 of 0.5/s): each cell engine
machine fed its science machine directly and also put its spare on a belt for the fifth science machine, and those
two inserters compete. Now a producer that cannot feed two consumers gets one consumer to itself (5 engine and 5
advanced-circuit machines for 5 science machines), producers are counted at full speed when clusters form, and a
leftover science machine gets a half cell. In game: 0.52/s, PASS (28 × 28, against 23 × 23 for the shared cells).
Clusters stay off by default.

## Factory packing

In the 2/s factory's stitch, blocks cover 59% of the box; of the other 20,481 tiles only 4,030 hold anything.
Whole padding rows hold nothing but poles, and there are large empty pockets (the sequence-pair packing's slack).
The squeeze keeps a change only if the factory shrinks, and each try re-routes everything, so it is time-bound.
This is the biggest lever left on factory size.

Seam carving (taking out a staircase line of empty or straight-belt tiles that weaves around blocks, where no whole
row is free) was prototyped on the 2/s preview: 2 rows and 1 column, about 1%. Corridor belts cross almost every
seam. Not built.

## Verifier with headroom

With outputs taken at their planned rate and machine counts rounded up, surplus backs up by design: inserters wait
for room on full belts, and a spare machine at the end of a belt can idle all the time (red 1.5/s: one assembler
finished nothing, output at plan). Neither fails the run any more; a short output still does.
