# Night log 2026-11-09: Py science 1 and 2 in game

Goal for the night: make a Py science factory produce in game, then try the next one. Every science at 0.02/s, jobs
in `jobs/py_sciences`, runs in `solves/py_*`, checked with `tools/factorio-verify/verify.py` (2 h game warm-up,
10 min measured: the game runs about 20x real time).

## Still open (morning)

  chain's barrels back up and leave by its overflow (0.013/s), while fillers on other chains have none. The test's
  overflow sink now also needs its own chain backed up (8 belts), which Py science 1 still passes with and which
  lifted logistic from 237 to 259 working machines; the barrels still leave, so the split of barrels between chains
  is what needs looking at.
- **Py science 2, latest**: the planner's integer program has a one-minute limit, so under load (two game instances,
  tests) the plan differs from run to run and `--resume` refuses the old split. The last fresh run stitched in five
  minutes (440 x 450) but with 4 validation errors in the corridors (a corridor belt feeding another's side), and with
  stop_after 1 the run ends there instead of trying another floorplan.
- **Overflows of items a block both makes and receives** still have no way out (39 in logistic).
- **Py science 2's stitch** with all overflows (117 links to the edge through the corridors) converges slowly: 519 then
  174 conflicts on the preview after 35 minutes a try.
- HeatTest and RouterFuzzTest failed once while two factory runs had every core (both are clock-bound; they passed
  on an idle machine all night).

## Stitching Py science 2

- **One port end walled in by construction** ("no path to 1 more connections", always exactly one): the stitch's
  obstacles walled all four sides of every pipe-to-ground, which joins on its open side only. A port that dived out
  beside another fluid's port walled in its neighbour's link end. With that fixed it routed at 453 x 391.
- **Routing time**: the next, bigger factory (22 blocks, 976 links) got 8 negotiation rounds in the fixed 6 minutes,
  conflicts 3469 -> 364 and falling by a quarter a round. Past 6 minutes it now goes on while it converges (10% over
  four rounds), 25 minutes at most, and the endgame keeps 8 more.
- Failed stitches and invalid factories are drawn into `<out>/failed_stitches/` with a text file (block places, the
  unreached tile and what walls it, the router's trace).

## Planner

- Burner fuel counts as used (fuel rods for a bhoddos culture went onto the mixed belt as a leftover).
- The exact re-solve read its solution after changing the program (stale zeros: Py science 1 had no machines), and
  flooring the slivers it brought in snowballed (63 recipes -> 819); slivers are now left out instead.
- Farms get a module their recipe takes (Py's breeding recipes take their own animal only: every reproductive
  complex had kmauts and stood at 0.01% speed), the plainest one first (special modules give +100% productivity).
- Loop carriers (items a loop only passes round one for one: heat bricks, barrels, cages) get no overflow and are
  seeded with one and a half crafts' worth; with `byproduct_overflow: "all"` (now set for the Py jobs) every product
  of a machine whose other products have users gets an overflow.

## Fluids, as the game sees them

- The exporter now writes recipes' module categories and, for a fluid a recipe has twice (uf6 at two enrichments),
  each one's box (`out:uf6/2`). Data re-exported.
- **Every pipe connection of a fluid box** is modelled, not only the first: a soil extractor passes water east to
  west, and a sulfuric acid pipe laid over its second connection filled with water. The fuel box's number now comes
  from the box (the glassworks' tar fuel box has two connections).
- A pipe-to-ground may come up on its machine connection facing the machine (a methanol reactor takes carbon sulfide
  and methanol on neighbouring tiles).
- Factorio 2.1 hands an unused box's connections to a used one (formic acid's one output box has all five output
  connections); the router keeps pipes off unused connections, so this is harmless.

## The in-game check (verify)

- Each machine's fluid boxes are reported with the tiles the game connects them on (`get_fluid_box_pipe_connections`
  in 2.1); `tools/factorio-verify/pipes.py` lists boxes joined to a pipe of another fluid (none now). `stall.py` and
  `why.py` count a fluid as short only when the box holds too little.
- By-product fluid exits hold their network at most half full (an overflow valve passes only above 80%, and a
  producer whose batch did not fit the last fifth of its box stood still).
- Overflow sinks take only real surplus (a maker of the item stands full); mixed belts always drain.
- Seeds: carriers once; seeds and catalysts a loop uses up are topped up through the warm-up whenever their machine
  is short, within five times the plan's use. Fluids seeded at a temperature are poured in at it.
