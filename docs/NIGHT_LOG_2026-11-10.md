## Morning (2026-11-11): two stitch bugs, then two factories

With the old router the production stitch came within six links on every floorplan, the same six links the guided
runs failed on ("goals reachable 0", "reach 1"). Neither router was to blame:

- **Belts walled in beside voids.** The stitch kept every tile beside a block's pipes and machines free of all corridor
  links (a corridor pipe there would join them). Since port ends belong to blocks, sinkholes and void pipes stand
  just outside them, and a belt port beside one had an inserter on one side of its first tile and the sinkhole's
  neighbours on the others. Those tiles are now kept from pipes only (`RouteProblem.pipeObstacles`).
- **Robot links starved.** The stitch dropped requester-fed ports from the factory's sources, so validation saw their
  belts fed by nothing: the first routed factory (996 x 880, routed in about 3 minutes) failed with 661 STARVED
  errors, all on items robots carry. They stay sources now; the harness and ports.json leave them out. A factory test
  with robot links covers it.
