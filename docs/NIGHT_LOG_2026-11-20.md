## The two late stops: sea sponge

The sea-sponge loop: the sponge farm grows sea sponge from sprouts, and the sprout maker needs one sea sponge a
craft to make the sprouts. Two trit makers outside the loop take sea sponge too (41 and 14 an hour planned). Under
`--loop-keep 2` the sprout maker asks for sponge only while it holds less than two crafts' worth: two sponges.
That is no reserve: it held two or three, did not count as short, and the trit makers took the sponges as they
came; at hour 17.7 the sprout maker had none, 52 lay parked on a belt it does not reach, and the farm (holding its
32 sponges as modules) had no sprouts. So the reserve has to be counted in time, not crafts (an hour of the
user's planned use, say): two crafts of a recipe that takes one item a craft is nothing, two crafts of one that
takes 15 coarse is an hour.

`tools/factory-flow` has the two item rules as settings now, off by default: `loop-keep` (crafts a loop's own users

## Afternoon hour (12:57 to 13:50): the reserve in time, and loop fluids in the mod

**`--loop-keep-time S`** (check) and the mod's **loop-keep-time** setting: a loop's own user also asks for the loop's
item while it has less than S seconds of its planned use. A slow machine cannot hold that: inserters fill it with
about two crafts, so the reserve has to lie in front of it. So the rule counts what lies on the belt tiles its
inserters pick from as well, and the user stops asking once a queue stands there (a tile with 4 or more of the
item). At S = 3600 the sprout maker wants 47 sponges. At hour 2.1, with the starters just ended, 1 of 21 loop inputs
counted as short in each of two runs. The sprout maker held 2 or 3 sponges with 8 or 9 queued in front of it.

**Loop fluids in the mod**: `tag.py` tags fluid loops as well (the same 24 fluids as the check on the margin
layout), and the mod's **loop-fluids** setting switches a fluid's users outside its loop off while the loop is
short. The check passes the setting under `--flow-mod`. So the player's blueprint now carries all three loop rules.
