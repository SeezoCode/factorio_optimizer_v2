## Py science 2: five faults, one after another

Every Py science 2 run stopped one to two hours after its starters ended, as on 2026-11-17. Each stop was traced
from the pack down; each cause was mended and the run started again. Four of the five were faults of the check or of
its rules, not of the factory.

1. **A reserve gate for fish on the fish breeder's fish-food belt.** The breeder (fish-food in 1, out 2) drops its
   fish food onto a belt that carries it round to its own input. A reserve gate for fish, an item that belt never
   carried, stood between the drop and the pickup, shut from the start: it opens only once the tile four back up the
   belt is full, and the breeder drops onto that tile from the side, so its back never filled. The breeder lived on
   its starters; when they ended it stood full of fish food, and the fish, the zipir eggs, the korlex milk and the
   packs stopped. Mended in the check and the mod: a gate also opens after its user's pickup tile has stood full for
   ten minutes (and stays open a minute), and at once while what stands at that tile is none of its item.
2. **Users outside a fluid loop soak it up.** The oleochemicals maker takes 10 bacteria a craft and gives back 11.
   `--loop-fluids` switches the users outside the loop off while it is short, but a switched-off machine still fills
   its input box from the pipe: the bacteria barreller and the resilin maker held 1,049 bacteria while the loop's
   maker stood with 6, and plastic and the packs behind it stopped. New in the check: `--loop-valves`, Py's overflow
   valve in place of the straight pipe at such a user's input, so it gets only what lies above 80% full in the pipe.
   34 of the 84 users outside fluid loops get one (the others sit on a junction or an underground pipe).
3. **An exit chest on the sap extractor's drop tile.** Py's sap extractor places its saps straight onto the tile
   below it, as a drill does. The check's overflow exit for the mulcher next to it put its chest there: every sap the
   extractor made (4,500 in four hours) went into that chest and was emptied by script, while the seed maker stood
   without. Mended: exits and fluid voids keep off every machine's drop tile. (24 machines on Py science 2 and 26 on
   logistic science place results directly; most drop onto bare ground, where one item lies and the rest stays in
   the machine for its inserters; a few mulchers drop onto a belt, which the planner does not know of.)
4. **The overflow port of a loop item's belt shut for good.** The third mulcher drops its fertilizer past the sap
   tree nursery, the belt's only user, toward the port. Fertilizer is a loop item, so the port stays shut (a rule
   made for the moss farm's catalyst): 330 fertilizer stood in front of it, the mulcher stood full, and a third of
   the saps the arqads need stopped with it. New in the check: `--loop-port-catalyst`, the port stays shut only for
   a catalyst (an item its maker also takes) or a carrier.
5. **Carriers parked away from their users.** With the four mends above, Py science 2 made 2 pack crafts in the
   hour after its starters (every earlier run: none) and stopped at hour 4 to 5 on barrels. The korlex milker, the
   geothermal water barreller and the arqad honey barreller stood without barrels or water barrels while 635 empty
   barrels and 900 water barrels lay on the belts, 263 and 780 of them on one network each, past their users (the
   milker's own belt held 25 just past its pickup). A gate past the last user of every item (`--reserve-items`)
   did not change it. This is finding 1 of the plan (lean belts park what passes a user), on a factory with far
   more carriers than logistic science; not mended tonight.

Without reserve gates (two runs, after mends 1 and 3) Py science 2 did worse: fertilizer parked past its user and
the animal chains behind it stood within an hour of the starters. So the gates help there, once they cannot lock.
Four hours of starters instead of two only moved the stop two hours later.

**What the new options do to logistic science** (margin layout, the passing rule set plus one option each, to hour
4): `--loop-valves` stops it (7, 8, 4, then no pack crafts an hour; the formic acid maker, outside the sulfuric acid
loop, sits behind a valve that opens only above 80% full, and pull keeps the pipes leaner than that). With
`--loop-port-catalyst` (13, 11, 12, 9) and `--reserve-items` (14, 11, 11, 10) it ran like the base rules. All three
together (e1) stopped at hour 3 as the valves alone did. So the valves are Py science 2's mend only; a version that
does not starve lean pipes is still to find (an underflow valve on the loop side, or a pump switched by the loop's
level).
