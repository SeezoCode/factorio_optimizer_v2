| q19 | q18 with four hours of starters | 7 7 5 0 6 0 | fish food, mukmoux, sap trees |

What was learned:

- **The bacteria loop has no margin in the plan.** Oleochemicals from skin takes 10 bacteria and gives 11; the 1 a
  craft (0.013/s) is exactly what resilin and the bacteria barreller are planned to take. Resilin sits behind a loop
  valve; the barreller hangs off a T one tile from the pipe, where no valve fits, and its box holds 1,000.
- **Loop valves only replaced a straight pipe at the user**: 33 of 84 outside users had a corner there. The valve
  now goes on the first straight tile along an unbranched pipe (5 more valves), and pull counts the short pipe behind
  a valve as the loop's pipe (without that the methanol makers never saw the bioplastic maker waiting, the pipe
  stood at 79% and the valve opens at 80%).
- **Long belts hold the loops' stock.** One fertilizer belt runs about 200 tiles underground and held 2,300
  fertilizer; filling it, fertilizer-3 ran at 170 times its plan under the belt-fill exception to the rate cap and
  took the biomass the fish breeder needed. The stops move from chain to chain (fish, mukmoux, saps, plastic) and are
  of this kind: stock in transit on belts hundreds of tiles long, and loops planned with nothing over. That needs the
  layout (shorter belts) and the plan (a margin on loops), not more rules.
