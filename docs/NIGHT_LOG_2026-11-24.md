### Why it fades

Traced in c1n, c1p, c1r and c1t at hours 3.5 to 4.5; the same chain each time:

1. **The fish loop.** The fish breeder (`breed-fish-4`) makes fish from eggs; the egg breeder (`breed-fish-egg-1`)
   makes eggs from fish. Fish also go to `full-render-fish-lard` (lard and meat). By the check's rule an item's
   loop is every user within three recipe steps of a maker: the egg breeder is one step back, the renderer two (by
   way of meat and fish food). Both count as the loop, so neither is held for the other: the renderer ran at 147 to
   168% of plan, the egg breeder stood without fish, the fish breeder without eggs (and `trits-4-dc`, outside, took
   eggs at 142% in c1p).
2. **Behind it, fish food and filtration media.** The fish breeder also makes the fish food the xyhiphoe cub maker
   needs; with the breeder stopped, no cubs, no nanofibrils, no filtration media by recipe 2. Filtration media from
   recipe 3 goes mostly to `coal-phenol` (273 to 278% of plan) and `molybdenum-filtration` (144 to 172%).
3. **Then the pack.** Molybdenum filtration stops, then molybdenum plate, crmoni, self-assembly monolayer and the
   pack. Advanced circuits fail the same way a step later (stainless steel by hot air needs aramid, which needs
   molybdenum plate).

The users far over plan (coal-phenol, `xyhiphoe-1-hot-cold` at 589 to 711%) did most of it during the warm-up, when
the starters fed everyone; by hour 4 the debug log shows them held, but the loop items are gone by then.

`--loop-tight` (new, check only): an item's loop is only its users at the fewest steps back to a maker. For fish
that puts the renderer outside, so it waits while the egg breeder stands short. On the 2% plan it also moves
users of seven other items outside (the fish breeder for filtration media, the xyhiphoe cub maker for fish food,
ash, biomass, seaweed, skin and bones users). In
the two runs with it (c1w on the 2% layout, c6d and c6e on the 3%), at hour 2 the breeder was not held by the
new rule (c6d: held by its filtration media quota with 15 eggs in it; c1w: short of eggs). When stopped at hour 2.7, c1w (the same warm-up as
c1t) had made 3 6 4 pack crafts by hour against c1t's 3 7 6, at 99% of plan over its last 1.7 hours; c6d made 6 5 0
and c6e 6 6 (hour 1.8). Too early to tell: the fish loop failed at hours 4 to 6 without it.
